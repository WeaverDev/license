LICENSE
=====

The full License License is a legally binding document. Software licenses, even those with the most noble of intentions, rely on the enforcement of copyright law. This means all legally recognized licenses are predicated on leveraging state power, thus violent coercion. The License License statement is a recognition of state violence, an agreement between the author of the software and those who wish to use and distribute it with the threat of legal force.

You may use any variation of the License License statement. Here is the full, long-form variant, which conveys the presence of license and a suggested ethic for distribution:

```
LICENSE

This software is released with a license.

Use, modify, or distribute it on the basis of legal protection.
```

Alternatively, you may use this form, which lacks such opinion:

```
LICENSE

This software is released with a license.
```

Your license may contain different content if you like. Choosing to include a license file is an excellent way to distribute your software with a License. A license statement such as this one may clarify intentions regarding the distribution of your work.
